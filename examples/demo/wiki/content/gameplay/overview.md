## Purpose

This category connects the game's intended [[Design/Player Experience|player experience]] to the systems that produce it during play.

## Structure

- [[Gameplay/Core Loop|Core loop]] defines the repeated sequence of actions, feedback, and consequences.
- [[Gameplay/Mechanics|Mechanics]] defines player verbs, rules, resources, and interactions.
- [[Gameplay/Progression|Progression]] defines how options, challenge, and understanding change over time.

## Design test

Gameplay belongs in the game when it strengthens a [[Design/Design Pillars|design pillar]] and creates a player decision or experience that can be observed in playtesting.
