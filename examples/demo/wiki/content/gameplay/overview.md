## Resolved foundation

> **Accepted** — The game is a **2D side-scrolling run-and-gun action platformer**. Its baseline play combines traversal, jumping, shooting, threat reading, evasion, and positioning.

The game may vary the intensity without changing that foundation:

- platforming-led traversal;
- enemy combat with manageable projectile patterns;
- concentrated “bullet hell” sequences;
- large authored set pieces such as a moving train or lift under attack;
- optional routes and discoveries.

These are encounter modes, not separate genres.

## Structural direction

> **Accepted** — The progression spine is an **authored campaign**. Handcrafted locations and set pieces remain stable enough to learn, master, revisit, and experience with different characters.

Selected supporting elements may include:

- persistent shortcuts through authored locations;
- a starting hub where post-death choices reopen;
- character or loadout changes after death;
- recoverable resources and weapon upgrades;
- abilities that expose optional routes and discoveries.

Procedural level generation and a complete randomized run reset are outside the current direction. “Roguelite” is therefore too ambiguous to name the structure; this wiki uses **authored campaign** and **death cycle** until the latter is precisely defined.
