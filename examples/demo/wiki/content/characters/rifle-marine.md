“Rifle Marine” is a gameplay role, not a decided personal name, appearance, biography, or permanent weapon restriction.

## Gameplay role

> **Accepted** — The Rifle Marine is an immediately understandable generalist with balanced movement, range, and survivability. It is the control case against which every contrasting character is evaluated.

The character must make the shared fundamentals satisfying without an exotic mechanic compensating for weak movement or combat.

## Ground movement

- One digital run speed
- Very quick acceleration
- Immediate or near-immediate reversal
- No analogue walk, sprint, or stamina state

## Jump

- One variable-height jump
- Moderate horizontal air control
- Faster fall than rise
- Short coyote-time and jump-input buffer
- No double jump, wall jump, ledge grab, or air dash

Exact values require playtesting.

## Innate capability: Combat Slide

> **Accepted** — A fast, low, ground-only horizontal commitment that passes beneath high projectiles and low obstacles.

- No invulnerability frames
- No contact damage
- Cannot reverse during commitment
- Uses the common character-ability input

## Aiming and firing

- Eight gameplay directions
- Primary gamepad binding uses quantized right-stick direction
- Directional input immediately fires the equipped weapon
- Optional grounded aim-lock plants the character and redirects fire
- Continuous-angle aiming is rejected

See [[Gameplay/Mechanics|Mechanics]] for cross-cutting input rules.

## Baseline survivability

> **Accepted** — Provisional five-segment integrity bar. Standard attacks remove one segment; heavy hazards may remove two. Damage produces clear feedback and short post-hit invulnerability.

## Baseline loadout

- [[Equipment/Baseline Rifle|Baseline Rifle]]
- No accepted armour or secondary item yet

## First identity evidence

> **Accepted** — The Freight Terminal rail-control system recognizes the Rifle Marine under an unknown designation officially recorded as killed in action. This does not resolve whether the character is original, cloned, reconstructed, copied, or associated with falsified data.

## Validation

> **Needs evidence** — Complete [[Missions/Restore the Cargo Line|Restore the Cargo Line]] and prove running, jumping, eight-direction firing, aim-lock, combat sliding, enemy reading, and camera behavior.

> **Needs image** — Define a readable original silhouette after gameplay dimensions and private starting assets are understood.
