RELAY is an institution-assigned operational codename, not a known personal name. When RAM reaches the crew, RELAY is trapped inside a hostile factory complex after sealing themself into its bunker. The RAM-required [[Missions/Breach the Lockdown|Breach the Lockdown]] Special Mission opens the facility and adds RELAY to the playable crew.

RELAY later leads [[Missions/Lift the Governor|Lift the Governor]], the Special Mission that reveals Overdrive. The player deploys primarily as RELAY, selects character activation rooms in any order, and temporarily controls each crew member through an unlimited-Overdrive trial.

## Gameplay role

> **Accepted** — RELAY controls space, machines, security, and enemy positioning through setup rather than high direct weapon output.

The player fantasy is to understand the infrastructure well enough to make the Encounter fight itself. Required internal interactions must remain authored and readable rather than becoming a universal “hack anything” verb.

## Character identity

Across all Specializations, RELAY retains:

- technical reading of machinery and security;
- average direct movement supplemented by utility;
- area control and enemy-position manipulation;
- access to lifts, cranes, conveyors, powered routes, and richer Memory Imprint information;
- evidence suggesting RELAY may remember building parts of the identity-control system.

The identity question is whether RELAY is uncovering the institution's network or recognizing work they once helped create.

## Representative Encounter

RELAY can hijack the Freight Terminal crane cycle, place a control node to divide the enemy formation, and activate machinery-linked optional routes. The Encounter remains completable through direct movement and combat.

## Presentation requirements

- Technical silhouette readable without relying on tiny gadget detail
- Deployables and controlled zones share a clear ownership language
- Hacking posture visibly communicates vulnerability and immobility
- Specialization variants distinguish balanced operation, distributed connection, and isolated electromagnetic force

> **Needs image** — Replace all card placeholders after deployable scale, effect language, and technical silhouette are validated.

> **Needs evidence** — Prove that setup creates readable tactical decisions without requiring pauses, menus, or hidden interaction rules during combat.
