RAM is an institution-assigned operational codename, not a known personal name. RAM enters the campaign during the second boss Encounter in [[Missions/Restart the Deep Pumps|Restart the Deep Pumps]], interrupting a stabilization state that ROOK or VECTOR cannot break. RAM joins the crew after helping the player finish the fight.

RAM then reports that RELAY has sealed themself inside a hostile factory bunker. Reaching the Systems Specialist requires [[Missions/Breach the Lockdown|Breach the Lockdown]], a Special Mission built to introduce RAM's destructive route language.

## Gameplay role

> **Accepted** — RAM enters dangerous close-range space and survives through aggression, interruption, stagger resistance, and environmental force.

Slow acceleration and limited long-range control make positioning commitments legible. High integrity must not turn incoming threats into irrelevant noise.

## Character identity

Across all Specializations, RAM retains:

- mass and high stagger resistance;
- a forward impact verb as the innate movement language;
- close relationship between attack recoil and movement;
- access to cracked barriers, heavy objects, and hazardous direct approaches;
- uncertain memories of surviving injuries that should have been impossible.

The identity question is whether RAM survived those injuries, inherited another soldier's trauma, or was given the memories to normalize disposable assignments.

## Specializations

> **In progress** — These cards define RAM's intended Specializations. Charge interruption, ricochet geometry, anchoring, projectile redirection, and environmental destruction require prototypes.

## Representative Encounter

RAM can break into the Freight Terminal machinery lane, interrupt the Shield Enforcer, push through hazardous timing windows, and use containers or walls as impact geometry. The central route remains completable without destruction.

## Presentation requirements

- Broad silhouette and clear center of mass
- Charge startup, armour direction, and interrupt window readable at gameplay scale
- Weapon recoil visibly connected to body movement
- Specialization variants distinguish direct breach, immovable siege, and unstable kinetic motion

> **Needs image** — Replace all card placeholders after charge and recoil prototypes establish readable mass.

> **Needs evidence** — Prove that RAM's durability enables commitment without removing the need to read hazards and enemy patterns.
