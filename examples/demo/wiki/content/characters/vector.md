VECTOR is an institution-assigned operational codename, not a known personal name. VECTOR is the crew voice that questions the killed-in-action match during ROOK's introduction Mission and becomes selectable when the player first reaches the Hub.

## Gameplay role

> **Accepted** — VECTOR wins through movement, precision, and routes other Characters cannot use comfortably. Low durability makes execution and threat reading more important than trading damage.

VECTOR should reveal elevated lines, concealed traversal markers, vents, cables, and maintenance approaches without making the central route impossible.

## Character identity

Across all Specializations, VECTOR retains:

- low durability and high movement expression;
- wall-based traversal as the innate route language;
- precision and weak-point incentives;
- access to elevated, narrow, or timing-intensive approaches;
- unusual recognition of locations VECTOR supposedly has never visited.

The central identity question is whether this familiarity comes from prior service, implanted reconnaissance data, copied memory, or repeated deployment in another body.

## Representative Encounter

VECTOR reads the Freight Terminal vertically: use cargo and wall surfaces to attack above the Shield Enforcer, cross timing windows without waiting for the central lane, and inspect concealed maintenance markers.

## Presentation requirements

- Narrow, immediately mobile silhouette
- Wall contact, aerial direction, and aim state readable at gameplay scale
- Precision equipment separated clearly from ROOK's general-purpose rifle profile
- Specialization variants distinguish phase instability, reconnaissance speed, and patient long-range control
