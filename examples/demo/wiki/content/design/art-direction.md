## Visual thesis

What should one screenshot communicate about the game?

## Readability

Define visual hierarchy for player, threats, affordances, effects, and environment.

## Shape, colour, and motion

Assign intentional roles to silhouette, palette, contrast, animation, and camera behavior.

## Production constraints

Specify asset scale, reuse strategy, target resolution, and quality bar only after the visual direction is understood.

## References

Reference images should explain **what principle is borrowed**, not merely establish mood.
