> **Accepted** — These are the canonical production filters. Their direction is decided; representative encounters must still validate that the game actually fulfills them.

## Familiarity without repetition

The game evokes the player's memory of 16-bit action games while presenting an original cast, world, and journey.

- **Requires:** readable archetypes and nostalgic visual and action vocabulary.
- **Rejects:** direct imitation and obscurity mistaken for originality.

> **Needs evidence** — Show that a player unfamiliar with the specific references still reads the characters and action correctly, while an experienced player feels recognition without seeing imitation.

## Character choice transforms play

Selecting another character changes how the player reads, approaches, and executes the same authored challenge.

- **Requires:** mechanically distinct decisions and capabilities.
- **Rejects:** cosmetic characters and minor statistical variation presented as different play styles.

> **Needs example** — Resolve one encounter using ROOK and VECTOR. Statistical variation alone does not satisfy this pillar.

## Discovery changes understanding and action

A meaningful discovery should alter both what the player believes and what the crew can do. Memory Imprints reveal identity or institutional evidence while granting a technique, tactical understanding, interaction, or route permission.

- **Requires:** Memory Imprints, meaningful routes, and mechanically consequential evidence.
- **Rejects:** passive lore collectibles and keycard gates that add no new action.

> **Needs example** — Show one Memory Imprint changing the interpretation of a known location and exposing a materially different action or route.

> **Needs evidence** — Confirm that Memory Imprints expand rather than erase each character's innate identity.

## Validation rule

An accepted pillar remains useful only while it has:

1. a concise statement;
2. observable consequences during play;
3. at least one feature it requires;
4. at least one tempting feature it rejects;
5. a practical playtest or example that could disprove it.
