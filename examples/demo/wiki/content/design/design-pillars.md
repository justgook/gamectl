## Candidate pillars

_To be derived from the intended player experience, not invented as marketing slogans._

Each pillar should contain:

1. A short, memorable statement.
2. What it means during play.
3. Design consequences.
4. A counterexample that violates it.

## Feature test

When considering a feature, ask which pillar it strengthens and how a playtest could demonstrate that effect.

## Non-pillars

Values such as “fun,” “polished,” and “good controls” are quality expectations, not differentiating pillars.
