## Campaign role

> **Accepted** — The Automated Fabrication Plant is the third campaign Zone and supports two sequential Special Missions:
>
> 1. [[Missions/Breach the Lockdown|Breach the Lockdown]] requires [[Characters/Ram|RAM]] and unlocks [[Characters/Relay|RELAY]].
> 2. [[Missions/Lift the Governor|Lift the Governor]] deploys primarily as RELAY and unlocks [[Gameplay/Overdrive|Overdrive]] for the entire crew.

These are explicit exceptions to the ordinary rule that main Missions remain completable by every Character and Specialization.

## Functional identity

The Plant is an automated industrial complex containing fabrication lines, material handling, security machinery, and a hardened control bunker. RELAY has sealed themself inside that bunker while hostile forces control or surround the wider facility.

The Zone should support:

- reinforced walls, pressure doors, and heavy moving objects for RAM;
- conveyors, cranes, fabrication arms, and security systems for RELAY;
- visible machinery state changes after RELAY is recovered;
- a deeper activation wing used by Lift the Governor;
- persistent shortcuts connecting the extraction route to the later activation route.

## Mission-state transformation

### During Breach the Lockdown

The Plant is hostile, physically sealed, and resistant to remote access. RAM must force an authored route through barriers, incoming industrial hazards, and projectile pressure. Completing the Mission opens the bunker and adds RELAY to the crew.

### During Lift the Governor

RELAY returns with enough access to enter the deeper activation wing. The facility branches into four character-specific trial rooms that can be completed in any order. Each room temporarily transfers player control while RELAY operates its system.

## Character reading on revisit

After both Special Missions, ordinary revisit content may allow all Characters and Specializations:

- ROOK handles mixed security and central production lanes;
- VECTOR reaches overhead service rails and isolated consoles;
- RAM opens force-reactive optional routes;
- RELAY reconfigures production and security machinery.

> **Open question** — Whether the Plant receives an ordinary post-Overdrive Mission or remains a Special Mission and exploration location.

## Concealed institutional value

Recovering RELAY has an obvious crew benefit. Activating the deeper system gives the crew Overdrive, but may also reconnect fabrication or identity-conditioning infrastructure valuable to OPERATOR's command structure.

The Mission should make empowerment and institutional danger occur together: the crew becomes stronger by activating a system it may later need to reject.

## Required content

- RAM route tutorial and extraction sequence
- RELAY bunker reveal
- Changed Plant state after extraction
- Branching Overdrive activation wing
- Four unlimited-Overdrive trials
- Clear separation between scripted control handoffs and ordinary deployment rules
