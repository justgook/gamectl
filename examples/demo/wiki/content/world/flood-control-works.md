## Campaign role

> **Accepted** — The Flood-Control Works is the second campaign Zone and supports two sequential Missions:
>
> 1. [[Missions/Open the Floodgates|Open the Floodgates]] ends with the first boss.
> 2. [[Missions/Restart the Deep Pumps|Restart the Deep Pumps]] advances deeper, ends with the second boss, and introduces [[Characters/Ram|RAM]].

Both Missions are ordinary deployments completable by either [[Characters/Rook|ROOK]] or [[Characters/Vector|VECTOR]] and, on later revisits, by every unlocked Character and Specialization.

## Functional identity

The Works regulates flood protection, drainage, and controlled water delivery for the nearby settlement. Its failure threatens occupied districts rather than serving as an abstract industrial inconvenience.

The Zone should contrast with the [[World/Freight Terminal|Freight Terminal]] through:

- vertical spillways and deep pump chambers;
- moving water levels and timed discharge hazards;
- suspended pipes, inspection platforms, and maintenance shafts;
- broad machinery lanes with alternate elevated approaches;
- persistent changes when gates and pumps return online.

## Character reading

- **ROOK:** reads stable machinery lanes, medium-range switches, and central combat spaces.
- **VECTOR:** reads spillway walls, suspended infrastructure, and narrow maintenance approaches.
- **RAM on revisit:** can force direct routes through damaged barriers and pressure doors.
- **RELAY on revisit:** can redirect pumps, gates, and security machinery for optional advantages.

No ordinary Mission route may make completion dependent on one Character or charged Overdrive.

## Persistent state

> **In progress** — Opening the floodgates and restarting the deep pumps should visibly and mechanically change later traversal. Exact water simulation, shortcuts, hazards, and revisit rewards require level design.

Likely persistent changes include:

- drained or filled chambers;
- active lifts and inspection platforms;
- newly stable settlement water flow;
- shortcuts created by restored pressure or machinery;
- optional evidence exposed by the changed water level.

## Concealed institutional value

The stated repairs create a genuine local benefit. The institution behind the Missions also regains access to high-capacity pumping, pressure regulation, and sealed industrial control infrastructure that may support the larger identity-control system.
