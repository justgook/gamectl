## Tactical role

> **Accepted** — Teach projectile-height reading, jumping, combat sliding, firing while moving, and deliberate advancement through a horizontal lane.

## Firing envelope

The sentry fires horizontally using two lanes:

- **Low shot:** barrel lowers; the projectile crosses ROOK's lower body. Jumping avoids it; sliding does not.
- **High shot:** barrel raises; the projectile crosses standing upper-body height. Combat sliding avoids it; jumping is unsafe or ineffective.

High and low are projectile lanes, not different aim directions.

## Telegraphs

- Deterministic sequence, never random
- Distinct barrel pose
- Warning animation
- Distinct sound
- Colour may reinforce but never solely communicate the shot

## Baseline placements

1. First placement teaches low shots only.
2. Second placement teaches high shots only.
3. Final placement uses a fixed low–high pattern with the cycling cargo crane.

See [[Gameplay/Representative Encounter|Representative Encounter]].

## Damage and defeat

> **Accepted** — A projectile removes one provisional integrity segment.

> **Open question** — Exact projectile speed, firing cadence, sentry durability, hit reaction, and death feedback require blockout evidence.

## Later combinations

The sentry may combine with hazards or advancing enemies after each pressure has been taught separately. Do not give the baseline sentry vertical, diagonal, tracking, or random fire.
