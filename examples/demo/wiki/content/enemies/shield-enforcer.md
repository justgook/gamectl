## Tactical role

> **Accepted** — Break stationary firing and force the player to move through, around, or over an advancing threat.

## Defence

Frontal armour completely blocks the [[Equipment/Baseline Rifle|Baseline Rifle]]. Blocked rounds:

- ricochet visibly and audibly;
- deal no damage;
- cause no stagger;
- never degrade or break the armour.

The rear remains vulnerable. Later special weapons may interact differently with armour, but the baseline rifle cannot.

## Advance and charge

1. Advance steadily toward the player.
2. Telegraph a committed horizontal charge at close range.
3. Remain unable to turn during the charge.
4. Pause after missing.
5. Expose the rear long enough for the player to reverse aim and attack.

The intended baseline answer is to jump over the charge, reverse direction, and fire into the rear. Combat slide has no invulnerability and cannot pass through the Enforcer.

## Damage

> **Accepted** — Contact or a melee strike removes one provisional integrity segment.

## Baseline placement

The first Enforcer appears alone in enough horizontal space to teach its rule. It is not combined with a sentry or crane in the flat baseline.

See [[Gameplay/Representative Encounter|Representative Encounter]].
