## Design questions

- What is the game's one-sentence pitch?
- What did the prototype prove about the intended game rather than only about its technology?
- What player experience should become the first design pillar?

## Production questions

- What is the smallest complete release target?
- Which prototype assets and systems survive the cleanup?
