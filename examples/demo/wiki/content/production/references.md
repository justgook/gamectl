## Game references

### Contra: Hard Corps

- **Useful:** a strong 16-bit action identity, immediately distinct selectable characters, confidence that character choice changes play, forward momentum, and memorable transitions through varied locations.
- **Avoid:** copying its characters, fiction, stages, exact mechanics, or visual assets; assuming nostalgia alone supplies a new game's identity.
- **Principle to adapt:** use familiar action-game language to create immediate anticipation, then fulfill it through an original cast, world, and character-specific play.

### Neon Abyss

- **Useful:** independent movement and firing through a second-stick shooting control that fires directly from directional input.
- **Adapt:** quantize gamepad and any mouse-directed input to the same eight gameplay directions.
- **Avoid:** allowing mouse precision or continuous analogue angles to create a mechanically different aiming model.

### Mega Man series

- **Useful:** precise action-platforming fundamentals, memorable authored challenges, readable enemies, ability mastery, and stages built around a focused mechanical idea.
- **Question:** whether its authored stage structure should be the main progression spine.

### Metroidvania structure

- **Useful:** optional discovery, abilities that reinterpret known spaces, route gating, and the pleasure of returning with new understanding.
- **Risk:** backtracking and continuous-world requirements can weaken the pacing and production focus of authored action set pieces.

### Rogue Legacy

- **Useful:** returning to a starting hub after death, reopening consequential choices, and turning failure into a changed next attempt.
- **Avoid:** assuming procedural level generation or randomized successors are required; the game retains an authored campaign.
- **Open question:** whether character selection, loadout, upgrades, routes, or some combination changes after death.

### Dark Souls and Bloodborne

- **Useful:** shortcuts as spatial mastery, meaningful checkpoints, dangerous resource recovery, and weapon investment.
- **Avoid:** importing difficulty, combat cadence, or opaque systems merely because the death structure is attractive.

### Blade Runner and Ghost in the Shell

- **Useful:** late-20th-century cyberpunk atmosphere, institutional power, industrial density, and questions about identity and technology.
- **Avoid:** copying plots or imagery, and avoid collapsing the direction into generic neon city dressing.

### Universal Soldier

- **Useful:** conditioned operatives who retain combat capability while personal identity, memory, autonomy, and institutional ownership are uncertain.
- **Avoid:** copying its military programme, characters, plot, or explanation for the operatives' condition.
- **Principle to adapt:** separate reliable operational knowledge from suppressed or manufactured autobiographical identity.

## Production asset sources

### CraftPix Cyberpunk Platformer collection

- **Source:** [Cyberpunk Platformer Asset Pixel Art](https://craftpix.net/sets/cyberpunk-platformer-asset-pixel-art/)
- **Useful:** coherent platformer tiles, characters, enemies, bosses, objects, backgrounds, and parallax layers for rapidly validating the freight-terminal encounter.
- **Adapt:** select and modify assets according to gameplay readability and the late-20th-century industrial cyberpunk direction.
- **Avoid:** allowing catalogue availability or bright-neon presentation to define the game; including every pack because it exists.
- **License constraint:** CraftPix permits licensed game use and modification but forbids redistribution of source or modified art. Raw licensed files must not enter the public repository. Its terms also forbid using licensed assets or derivatives for AI training, testing, validation, or improvement; do not provide the art to AI tools. See the [CraftPix file license](https://craftpix.net/file-licenses/).
- **Open verification:** confirm that the intended packaged browser distribution complies when art files remain technically extractable.

## Reference rule

For every reference, state:

- what specifically is useful;
- what does not fit this game;
- the principle to adapt rather than the surface feature to copy.

References are inspiration and evidence, not decisions.
