## Briefing

> **Accepted** — Enter the locked [[World/Automated Fabrication Plant|Automated Fabrication Plant]], reach its hardened control bunker, and extract [[Characters/Relay|RELAY]].

RAM knows RELAY is inside after joining the crew during [[Missions/Restart the Deep Pumps|Restart the Deep Pumps]]. RELAY has sealed themself into the bunker while hostile forces control or surround the wider Plant.

## Special deployment rule

> **Accepted** — This Mission requires [[Characters/Ram|RAM]] and initially deploys RAM's default **Breacher** Specialization. It is an explicit Special Mission exception to universal Character and Specialization compatibility.

The requirement must be visible before deployment. The Mission exists to teach RAM's route, mass, interruption, and recoil language—not to surprise the player with an invalid choice.

> **Open question** — Whether later replay permits RAM's other Specializations or every Character after the bunker has been opened persistently.

## Objective sequence

1. Deploy as RAM at the Plant perimeter.
2. Break the first lockdown barrier.
3. Learn charge timing against industrial hazards and incoming fire.
4. Redirect or destroy moving containers and breach-reactive walls.
5. Advance through the fabrication floor toward the control bunker.
6. Break the final hardened seal.
7. Reach RELAY and establish why they sealed themself inside.
8. Extract or secure the bunker.
9. Return to the Hub with RELAY available.

## Required RAM trials

The Mission should teach through authored problems rather than instruction text:

- ram an incoming container or barrel out of the route;
- breach a damaged wall under pressure;
- redirect an incoming large projectile with a precisely timed impact;
- use close-range force without treating RAM's integrity as unlimited;
- distinguish breakable RAM-reactive structures from ordinary scenery.

Exact verbs and ordering require a prototype.

## Local benefit

- Recovers a living crew member.
- Removes the immediate threat surrounding RELAY's bunker.
- Opens the Plant for later exploration and activation.

## Concealed purpose

OPERATOR's command structure gains both RELAY's technical capability and physical access to a sealed industrial facility. Whether OPERATOR knowingly sent RAM to recover a former system architect remains unresolved.
