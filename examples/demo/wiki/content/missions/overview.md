A **Mission** is an objective-driven deployment into a [[World/Overview|Zone]]. A Zone can support more than one Mission, and a Mission can contain multiple Encounters.

## Accepted Missions

- [[Missions/Restore the Cargo Line|Restore the Cargo Line]] — introduction Mission in the [[World/Freight Terminal|Freight Terminal]].

## Page rule

Every accepted Mission receives one page containing:

- briefing and stated objective;
- actual local benefit;
- concealed institutional purpose;
- target Zone and required Encounters;
- entry, success, failure, and return conditions;
- discoveries and progression consequences;
- unresolved narrative or gameplay work.
