## Briefing

> **Accepted** — Restart the deep pumping system so the restored floodgates can sustain safe water control instead of providing only temporary relief.

The stated objective is truthful. The first Mission opened controlled release; this Mission restores the machinery required to maintain it.

## Campaign role

> **Accepted** — The player deploys as [[Characters/Rook|ROOK]] or [[Characters/Vector|VECTOR]], advances deeper into the [[World/Flood Control Works|Flood-Control Works]], and reaches the second boss. [[Characters/Ram|RAM]] intervenes during that Encounter and joins the crew after victory.

This remains an ordinary Mission until the authored boss intervention. No selectable Character or Specialization may be invalidated before that moment.

## Objective sequence

1. Return to the changed Flood-Control Works.
2. Use the restored upper gates to reach the deep pump chambers.
3. Activate supporting pressure and power systems.
4. Reach the primary pump core.
5. Fight the second boss as ROOK or VECTOR.
6. Trigger the boss's clearly telegraphed repair/stabilization state.
7. Witness RAM break the stabilizer and expose the boss.
8. Resume player control and complete the victory.
9. Restart the deep pumps and return to the Hub with RAM available.

## Second boss and RAM intervention

> **Accepted** — The boss enters a deliberate closed repair or stabilization state that ROOK and VECTOR cannot interrupt. The state must be communicated as machinery performing an authored process, not unexplained regeneration or an unfair damage check.

RAM arrives and physically destroys or dislodges the stabilizing mechanism. The intervention demonstrates RAM's force language and creates the opening, but the player remains responsible for finishing the boss.

> **Open question** — Boss identity, exact trigger threshold, whether RAM remains visible during the final phase, and how much control returns immediately after the intervention.

## Local benefit

- Restores sustainable drainage and pressure regulation.
- Makes the settlement benefit from Open the Floodgates durable.
- Opens deeper maintenance and revisit routes inside the Zone.

## Concealed purpose

Restarting the pumps reconnects another industrial subsystem to OPERATOR's command structure. The exact relationship between pressure infrastructure and the concealed identity project remains unresolved.

## Deployment and outcomes

- **Origin:** [[World/Hub|Hub]]
- **Target Zone:** [[World/Flood Control Works|Flood-Control Works]]
- **Available Characters:** ROOK or VECTOR on first completion; all unlocked Characters on revisit
- **Required Character:** none before the scripted intervention
- **Required Overdrive:** none
- **Success:** restart the pumps, unlock RAM, and expose [[Missions/Breach the Lockdown|Breach the Lockdown]]
- **Failure or withdrawal:** use the standard Hub return cycle; exact resource consequences remain unresolved

## Transition to RELAY

After the boss, RAM reports that [[Characters/Relay|RELAY]] has sealed themself inside a hostile factory bunker. The bunker cannot be reached through the crew's current movement or technical access; RAM can breach it directly.
