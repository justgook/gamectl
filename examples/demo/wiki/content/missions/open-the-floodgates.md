## Briefing

> **Accepted** — Restore the upper flood-control gates before uncontrolled pressure threatens occupied settlement districts.

The objective is truthful. Completing the Mission restores a real protective and water-distribution function while opening the crew's route deeper into the [[World/Flood Control Works|Flood-Control Works]].

## Campaign role

> **Accepted** — This is the first full post-Hub Mission. The player deploys as [[Characters/Rook|ROOK]] or [[Characters/Vector|VECTOR]] with one unlocked Specialization and compatible equipment.

The Mission must prove that Hub selection produces materially different approaches without punishing the player's first informed Character choice.

## Objective sequence

1. Deploy from the [[World/Hub|Hub]] into the upper Works.
2. Read the failed gate and pressure-control state.
3. Advance through central machinery lanes and elevated maintenance alternatives.
4. Restore or manually release the primary gate controls.
5. Defeat the first boss.
6. Confirm controlled water flow and reduced settlement danger.
7. Return to the Hub with access to the deeper Works unlocked.
